# Scoring rubric and action guide

Read this file in Step 4 when the register is empty, and in Step 6 to score assets and build the action list.

## Four checks, 25 points each

Each asset gets a score out of 100, made of four checks grouped into three evidence pillars.

| Pillar | Check | 25 points | Partial credit | 0 points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proof of ownership | Receipt | A receipt, invoice or order confirmation matched to the asset by serial number, or by brand and model | 15: matched by item name only, or no price shown | No receipt found |
| Proof of condition | Dated photo | A photo that clearly shows the asset and has an EXIF capture date | 10: photo without a capture date, or the asset only partly visible | No photo found |
| Proof of condition | Serial number | A serial number readable in a photo, or printed on a receipt or warranty | 10: model number only | Neither found |
| Coverage | Coverage | The policy covers it (named in the schedule or clearly inside a covered category) | 10: conditional (above a single-item limit, a possible exclusion, business use under a home policy, or an ended policy period) | Excluded, not stated, or no policy document found |

A single dated photo of the asset's serial plate earns both the Dated photo and the Serial number checks, because it proves the item was in the user's possession on that date.

A check whose step failed scores 0 and shows "not checked", not "missing". The report must then be titled "Partial audit".

## Bands

| Band | Score |
| --- | --- |
| High | 80–100% |
| Medium | 40–79% |
| Critical | 0–39% |

Worked example: an espresso machine named in the policy, with no receipt and no photo, scores 25% (Critical). After the user saves one dated photo of its serial plate, it scores 75% (Medium). Once the receipt is saved, it scores 100% (High).

## Diagnosis line

Format: `Asset name: score%. Finding; finding.` Name what is missing, not what is present, unless the asset is at 100%.

- `Café espresso machine: 25%. No receipt found; no serial number photo.`
- `MacBook Pro 14: 85%. Photo has no capture date.`
- `Wedding ring: 50%. Value 4,200 is above the 1,500 single-item jewelry limit; no serial or hallmark photo.`

## Actions for each gap

Suggest destination folders under the first scan folder: `Receipts/YEAR`, `Assets/CATEGORY` and `Insurance`. Use the real purchase year and category when they are known. Suggest folders only; the Skill never creates them.

| Gap | Action |
| --- | --- |
| No receipt | Find the receipt or order confirmation for the asset (check the email account or online store it was bought from, or ask the seller for a copy) and save it to `Receipts/YEAR`. |
| Receipt matched by name only | Save a receipt or order page that shows the brand and model to `Receipts/YEAR`. |
| No dated photo | Take a photo of the asset where it is normally kept and save it to `Assets/CATEGORY`. Photos taken with a phone carry their date automatically. |
| Photo without a date | Take a new photo with a phone or camera that records the date, and save it to `Assets/CATEGORY`. |
| No serial number | Photograph the serial number plate (usually on the back, the underside or inside the battery or lid area) and save it to `Assets/CATEGORY`. |
| Coverage not stated | Ask the insurer to confirm in writing that the asset is covered, or add it to the schedule, and save the confirmation to `Insurance`. |
| Above a single-item limit | The value exceeds the limit quoted in the policy. Ask the insurer about scheduling the item separately. |
| Business use under a home policy | Ask the insurer whether business equipment is covered, or whether a business policy is needed. |
| Excluded | Quote the exclusion. Ask the insurer about an add-on or a separate policy. |
| Policy period ended | Save the current policy documents to `Insurance`, then run the audit again. |
| No policy document found (all assets) | Save the current insurance policy and schedule to `Insurance`. This is always the first action. |
| Dated photo older than 3 years | Take an updated photo. Low priority; no points are lost. |

Starter actions when the register is empty: save insurance documents to `Insurance`, save receipts for valuable items to `Receipts/YEAR`, and photograph valuable items and their serial plates into `Assets/CATEGORY`.

## Ranking

Rank actions by priority = points gained × value weight.

- Value weight: 3 if the asset value is 2,000 or more, 2 if 500 to 1,999 or unknown, 1 if below 500.
- Ties: Critical assets first, then the quicker action (a photo before finding a receipt, and a receipt before contacting the insurer).
- The "no policy document found" action always comes first.

Show each action's effect, for example `+50 points: 25% → 75%`. Combine the photo and serial number actions into one action when a single serial-plate photo would earn both checks.
